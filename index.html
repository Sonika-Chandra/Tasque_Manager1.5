Connect your Google Cloud project

Google only lets an app read Gmail and Classroom after the app is registered. It's free and takes about 10 minutes:

  1. Create a project at console.cloud.google.com.
  2. Turn on the Gmail API and the Google Classroom API.
  3. Set up the OAuth consent screen (External, Testing) and add your email as a test user.
